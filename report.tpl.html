What this measures

Pitwall reads your local Claude Code session logs and reconstructs, minute by minute, what every agent was doing and where your attention was. The model comes from human supervisory control of multiple robots: the field that answered "how many machines can one person run" long before coding agents existed.

Day by day

Each chart counts agents running in parallel: height is the number of agents working at that moment, one shade per agent deep. Blue ticks underneath are your prompts. Stippled grey is dead air, when everything had finished and nothing was queued. The clock skips your breaks.

Every project's total attention, split three ways. Efficiency is the share of that attention that was an agent actually working; the rest was you attending it, or the agent waiting for you. Ranked by agent work. Click a row for its sessions.
