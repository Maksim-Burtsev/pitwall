# Logo generation prompts

Same family as Punchcard and Ponytail: rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on white, careless
doodle linework, ink splatter and stray dots, monochrome. The character is the race engineer
on the pit wall: the one person who watches the whole fleet, with a headset on and a stopwatch
in hand, and who decides when each car comes in.

## Avatar / logo (`assets/logo.png`)

> Rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on white background, careless doodle style with ink
> splatters and stray dots, monochrome only. Portrait of a race engineer on the pit wall:
> mid-40s, short hair flattened by a radio headset with a thin boom microphone in front of the
> mouth, a stopwatch on a lanyard around the neck. Calm, focused, slightly narrowed eyes looking
> straight at the viewer, the look of someone who has just read the whole timing screen in one
> glance. Team jacket with the collar up. No logos, no readable text anywhere, no background
> details.

## Banner (`assets/banner.png`)

> Wide horizontal composition, rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on white, careless doodle style
> with ink splatters, monochrome only. A race engineer at the pit wall, half-length, slightly
> left of center: radio headset with a boom microphone, stopwatch in one hand, the other hand
> resting on a small desk with two monitors seen from behind (no readable screens). Behind him,
> hinted with a few loose strokes, a row of three or four small parked cars in a pit lane,
> waiting. On the right, large hand-lettered "PITWALL" in rough ink capitals, and below it in
> smaller handwriting drawn with the same pen and the same wobbly, uneven strokes, clearly the
> same hand and not a typeface: "Your agents are waiting for you." Plenty of white space, no
> other text, no frame, no border.
